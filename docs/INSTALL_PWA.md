# Install the app on your phone

The app is the same website installed to the home screen. No app store is involved. Use the live HTTPS link.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open the site in Chrome.
2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
3. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
4. Open it from the home screen: it runs full screen with its own icon.

## iPhone (Safari)

1. Open the site in **Safari** (not inside another app).
2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open it from the home screen.

An installed iPhone app keeps its own login, so sign in again inside it.

The same steps, with a button where the browser supports one, are on the site at `/install`.
